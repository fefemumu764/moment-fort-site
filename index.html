Moment Fort

The best moments, in 60 seconds.

Moment Fort is a TikTok account that shares short highlight clips from YouTube videos. Every clip comes from a creator who has given us permission to clip their videos, and every post credits the creator and links to the full video.

How it works

  1. We follow a list of YouTube channels whose creators have authorized us to make clips.
  2. Our desktop tool finds the most-replayed moments of a new video and turns them into short vertical clips with subtitles.
  3. Using TikTok's official Content Posting API, the tool publishes each clip to the Moment Fort TikTok account only, with a caption that credits the creator and links to the source.

TikTok integration

The tool is used only by the owner of the Moment Fort TikTok account. It uses Login Kit so the account owner can authorize the tool once, and the Content Posting API (scope video.publish) to post clips to that account. It does not access, collect or post to any other TikTok user's account.

For creators

Want your videos clipped, or want us to stop? Contact us and we will add or remove your channel right away.
