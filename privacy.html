Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains what data the Moment Fort tool (the "Service") handles and why. The Service is used only by the owner of the Moment Fort TikTok account.

1. Data we receive from TikTok

When the account owner authorizes the Service with TikTok Login Kit, TikTok provides:

  • an access token and a refresh token, used only to publish clips to the authorized account;
  • basic account information (open ID, display name, avatar) through the user.info.basic scope, used only to confirm which account is connected;
  • the account's posting options (allowed privacy levels, comment/duet/stitch settings, posting limits), used only to post with valid settings;
  • the status of each upload, used to confirm that a clip was published.

2. How the data is stored

Tokens are stored on the account owner's own computer, or as encrypted secrets in the owner's private automation environment. They are never shared, sold or used for any other purpose.

3. Data we do not collect

The Service does not collect data about other TikTok users, viewers or followers. The website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the Service is in use. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services), after which the tokens stop working and are deleted.

5. Third parties

Clips are published through TikTok's official API; TikTok's own Privacy Policy applies to content on TikTok.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
